Admissions

Enrollment Process

A clear, simple path to becoming a BS Mathematics student

How to Apply

Step-by-Step

01

Submit Application

Visit the ISPSC Admissions / Registrar's Office or go online to ispsc.edu.ph. Fill out the application form and submit the required documents.

02

Take Examination

Schedule and take the ISPSC College Admission Test. The exam covers core mathematics and critical thinking skills.

03

Get Results

Wait for your examination results. Results will be communicated by the Admissions Office through your registered contact information.

04

Confirm Your Slot

Upon passing, you will be notified to confirm your enrollment slot. Limited positions are available, so act promptly!

05

Enroll

Complete your enrollment by submitting any remaining documents and confirming your academic details with the Registrar.

06

Start Classes

Welcome to ISPSC! Attend your orientation and begin your exciting journey toward a degree in Mathematics.

Preparation

Required Documents

Make sure you have all the necessary papers ready before heading to the Admissions Office. Missing documents may cause delays in your application.

Completed Application Form
Senior High School Diploma / Certificate of Graduation
Official Transcript of Records (showing GWA)
Two (2) Recent Passport-Size Photos
PSA-Certified Birth Certificate
Valid Government-Issued Identification
Certificate of Good Moral Character

Scholarship Info

DOST-SEI Scholarship

Financial assistance available for qualifying students. Covers additional costs beyond what RA 10931 provides.
