Program Overview

Designed for
Future Mathematicians

The BS Mathematics program at ISPSC is tailored for academically strong individuals who are passionate about analytical thinking. Over four years, students dive deep into foundations, major courses, electives, and a capstone research project that transforms theory into real-world solutions.

  • Senior High School graduates from STEM or related tracks
  • Minimum General Weighted Average (GWA) of 85%
  • Must pass the ISPSC College Admission Test
  • Individuals eager to develop advanced analytical skills
