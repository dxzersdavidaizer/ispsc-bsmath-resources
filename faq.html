Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before applying

Admission & Prerequisites

Applicants must be Senior High School graduates, preferably from the STEM strand, with a General Weighted Average (GWA) of 85% or higher. They must also pass the ISPSC College Admission Test.

While the STEM strand is preferred, applicants from related academic tracks may still be considered, provided they meet the 85% GWA requirement and pass the admission test.

The admission test is held on specific dates announced by the Admissions Office. We recommend visiting ispsc.edu.ph or contacting the Registrar's Office for the latest schedule.

Cost & Scholarships

Under Republic Act No. 10931 (Universal Access to Quality Tertiary Education Act), tuition and fees at ISPSC are covered. Additionally, qualifying students can benefit from the DOST-SEI Scholarship, which provides further financial support.

The DOST-SEI (Department of Science and Technology €“ Science and Education Institute) scholarship supports deserving students in science and mathematics programs. Applicants must meet academic and financial eligibility criteria. Details are available at the ISPSC Admissions Office.

Program Structure

The program is a 4-year full-time course. Students progress through Foundation Courses, Major Courses, Electives, and a Capstone/Research project in their final year.

The BS Mathematics program is conducted in-person at the ISPSC campus in Tagudin, Ilocos Sur. Classes are held Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM €“ 5:00 PM.

Enrollment & Process

Typical required documents include your Senior High School diploma or certificate of graduation, official transcript of records showing your GWA, a completed application form, and valid identification. Contact the Admissions Office for the complete checklist.

Enrollment deadlines are announced by the ISPSC Registrar's Office. We encourage applicants to visit ispsc.edu.ph or call (077) 652-2093 for the most up-to-date enrollment timeline.

Career & Outcomes

BS Mathematics graduates are well-prepared for careers in teaching (secondary and tertiary levels), data analysis, operations research, government service, financial analytics, and academic research. The program's capstone and internship components further strengthen employability.
